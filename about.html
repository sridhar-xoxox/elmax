Why ELMAX ELECTRICALS?

Safety is Priority #1

Govt. Licenced - "A Grade" - Electrical Contractor (EA-2473)

“ELMAX ELETRICALS” is exclusively in the fields of Industrial and Multi-storied Building Electrical construction for both LT and HT industries. We are one of the active and leading Electrical Contractors, Materials Suppliers, and Panel Board Suppliers. We also provide liaison services with the Tamil Nadu Electricity Board and the Electrical Inspectorate of Tamil Nadu.

The head of our organization is a Qualified Electrical Engineering Graduate with professional experience in the electrical construction field for over 20 years. They are backed up by a Supervisor and Contractor competency certificate from the Electrical Licensing Board of the Government of Tamil Nadu.

We are well-equipped with all facilities for on-site testing of our installations towards quality assurance. We have excellent CAD assistance for our design works, and are registered with government agencies like GST, IT, Group Insurance, etc. We have extensive experience in electrical infrastructure projects for automobile manufacturing, hotels, hospitals, textiles, steel, alloys, pharma, IT, SEZ, communications, food processing, FMCG, and other industries.
