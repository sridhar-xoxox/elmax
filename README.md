## 📌 About

**ELMAX Electricals** is a government-licensed **"A Grade" Electrical Contractor (EA-2473)** and electrical materials supplier providing industrial, commercial, and residential electrical solutions across Tamil Nadu.

With **25+ years of experience** and **500+ completed projects**, ELMAX focuses on delivering reliable electrical infrastructure, quality products, and complete project execution.

## 🛠️ Our Services

### 1. Industrial Power

High-capacity electrical infrastructure for factories and industrial facilities, including:

* Switchgear installation
* Transformer installations
* Industrial power distribution
* Electrical infrastructure for plants and factories

### 2. Wiring & Installation

Complete electrical wiring and installation services for commercial and residential projects.

* Internal wiring
* Conduit laying
* Panel board installation
* Electrical fit-outs
* Electrical system installation

### 3. Turnkey Projects

End-to-end electrical project execution covering the complete project lifecycle.

**Design → Procurement → Installation → Commissioning**

Suitable for projects including:

* Hospitals
* Textile mills
* Manufacturing facilities
* MSBs
* Commercial buildings

### 4. Additions & Alterations

Electrical system upgrades and extensions designed to support increasing industrial and commercial power requirements.
